\section*{Data Availability}

Boston neighborhood influenza-like illness rates are published by the Boston
Public Health Commission. Chicago ZIP code influenza-like illness activity
levels are published by the Chicago Department of Public Health on the City of
Chicago Data Portal (dataset 8vvr-jv2g; ZIP code boundaries, dataset
unjd-c2ca). Columbus data
were provided by Columbus Public Health as de-identified visit-level extracts;
with their agreement, the weekly area-level counts used in every analysis are
publicly available with the code, and the visit-level extracts are available
from Columbus Public Health on request. Weather data come from the Open-Meteo
archive (CC BY 4.0), and boundary files from the City of Boston, the City of
Chicago, and the US Census Bureau. All code, frozen model
configurations, and the scripts that produce every table and figure are
available at \url{https://github.com/CodeMateo15/GNN-Influenza-Boston}
\NOTE{tag the release used for this paper, e.g., paper-v1, and cite it or a
Zenodo DOI here}. The forecasting models are also released as an installable
Python package for health departments, areaFluForecast
(\url{https://github.com/CodeMateo15/areaFluForecast}), which reproduces the
Boston results reported here \NOTE{confirm once the parity check (Gate B)
passes; add the package's Zenodo DOI}.
