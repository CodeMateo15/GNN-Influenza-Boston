Dear Editors,

 

We would like our manuscript, “Integrating Time, Weather, Demographics, and Geography: Graph Neural Networks for Neighborhood-Level Influenza Forecasting in Three US Cities,” to be considered as an Original Contribution for AJE’s special collection on Artificial Intelligence in Health.

 

Health departments act on influenza neighborhood by neighborhood; however, most forecasts cover states or regions. We forecast emergency department visits for influenza-like illness 1, 2, and 4 weeks ahead in Boston’s 14 neighborhoods and repeated the analysis in Columbus and Chicago. Our graph neural network integrates temporal, environmental, demographic, and geospatial information.

 

We believe our work will interest AJE’s readers because it offers a rigorous test of whether neighborhood-level influenza forecasts are accurate enough to guide local public health action.

 

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, all authors have approved it, and none of the authors has a conflict of interest to declare. If the manuscript is accepted, we intend to publish open access, with article processing charges covered through Northeastern University Library’s agreement with Oxford University Press.

 

Thank you for your consideration
